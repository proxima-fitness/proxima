# Proxima Fitness — Product marketing context

Use this when writing or editing marketing copy so all changes stay on-brand and audience-aligned.

## Page purposes and primary actions

| Page / Route | Purpose | Primary action |
|--------------|---------|----------------|
| **Home** (`/`) | Landing for the program builder; visitor may not know Proxima. | Start a chat and create a workout program (no signup required to try). |
| **Proxima app** (`/proxima-app`) | Full product story: AI coach, programs, PRs, themes, integrations, social proof, FAQ. | Learn about the product and move toward signup or building a program. |
| **Waitlist** (`/waitlist`) | Andy (agentic workout builder for Hevy) early access. | Join waitlist with email. |
| **About** (`/about`) | Founder story and mission. | Build trust; support signup or exploration. |
| **Pricing** (`/pricing`) | Plan comparison and upgrade path. | Choose a plan or upgrade. |

## Audience

- **Primary:** Intermediate lifters who follow or want to run structured programs (bodybuilding, powerlifting, strength, etc.).
- **Secondary:** Hevy app users who want AI-generated programs and exports; coaches who want to share programs and grow a following.
- **Problems they have:** Plateauing, lack of time to design programs, analysis paralysis, wanting proven programs (e.g. from Reddit) or custom plans without hiring a coach.
- **Language they use:** Programs, PRs, gains, plateau, RPE, %1RM, Hevy, export, follow a program, build a program.

## Product / offer

- **What it is:** AI-powered workout program builder that generates tailored programs via chat; exports to Hevy (and PDF, spreadsheet); profile with PRs and social features; themes (light, dark, Classic Blue, Pastel Pink).
- **What makes it different:** Expert-trained AI for fitness; works inside your existing stack (e.g. Hevy); free tier is generous; community programs and coach-sharing.
- **Key transformation:** From "I don't know what to run" or "I'm stuck" to a clear, personalized program they can run in their favorite app.
- **Proof points:** 4.5 on App Store; member/workout/program stats (use current numbers); testimonials (real quotes in `feedback` constant).

## Voice and tone

- **Formality:** Professional but friendly; conversational where it fits.
- **Personality:** Confident, helpful, no hype. Bold headlines; clear, benefit-led body copy.
- **Spelling:** US English for broad audience (e.g. "personalized," "favorite" unless intentionally UK).

## Context for traffic

- **Home:** Often organic or direct; may be first touch. Copy should stand alone.
- **Proxima app:** Deeper look; can assume some interest in fitness apps or AI.
- **Waitlist:** Hevy users or people who already know Proxima/Andy; can be more specific and benefit-dense.
