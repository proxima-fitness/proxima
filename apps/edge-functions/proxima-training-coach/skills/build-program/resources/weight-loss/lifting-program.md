# Weight Loss: Lifting program design

Principles below synthesize common **evidence-based coaching** themes (e.g. prioritizing training stimulus and recovery during energy restriction) with the muscle-group volume ideas in `bodybuilding.md`. They are **food for thought or places to start**, not dogmatic rules to follow to the letter.

For **nutrition and habits**, see `diet.md`. For **cardio volume and same-day ordering** with weights, see `cardio-exercise.md`. For **exercise names and weekly set landmarks (MEV / MAV / MRV)**, use `bodybuilding.md` when selecting movements from `list_exercises`.

---

## Purpose of lifting on a cut

- **Primary job of the lifting program:** preserve **lean mass** and **training quality** while you run a calorie deficit. Scale weight is driven mainly by **average energy balance** and adherence-see `diet.md`-not by “burning fat” in the weight room.
- **Do not drop resistance training** to chase extra cardio: lifting supports muscle retention and long-term metabolic robustness; cardio complements that-see `cardio-exercise.md`.

---

## Relationship to hypertrophy programming

- Treat **`bodybuilding.md` as the default library** for rep ranges, volume landmarks per muscle, compound RPE guidance, and exercise categories when building the week.
- **Cut-specific adjustment:** your **recovery budget** is shared across lifting, **caloric deficit**, **extra cardio**, sleep, and life stress. If something has to give, **reduce fluff volume** (e.g. redundant isolation or sets far above MEV) before you drop **hard sets on compounds** or whole sessions-unless joint health or schedule forces otherwise.

---

## Volume and frequency

- Aim for a **sustainable weekly stimulus**: often **near your prior meaningful training volume**, or roughly from **MEV toward the lower end of MAV** per muscle rather than pushing **MRV** while dieting-unless you already tolerate high volume and recovery stays solid.
- **Research note:** studies comparing **moderate vs higher** weekly sets during a deficit sometimes find **similar** lean-mass outcomes when protein is adequate and effort is consistent; results vary by population (e.g. trained males vs females), deficit size, and prior volume. **Consistency and adequate stimulus** usually matter more than “max volume at all costs.”
- **Frequency:** pick a split you can repeat for weeks-**full body**, **upper/lower**, **PPL**, etc.-based on **days per week**, equipment, and which layout produces **good sessions** (not grinding junk volume). No single split wins for everyone.

---

## Intensity and RPE

- Follow the **compound RPE cap** spirit from `bodybuilding.md` (typically **~8.5 RPE** or **1-2 reps in reserve** on heavy compounds in normal hypertrophy blocks). Dieting is a bad time to turn every set into a true **max effort**.
- **Autoregulate:** if sleep, hunger, or stacked cardio crush performance, keep technique crisp and **leave reps in the tank** rather than grinding compensations.

---

## Progression expectations

- On a long cut, **strength and reps may plateau or dip slightly**. Prioritize **form**, **controlled progression** (double progression, small load jumps), and **session-to-session consistency** over forcing PRs.
- If loads stall but you are losing weight on trend, **maintaining** loads can still be a win.

---

## Deloads and fatigue

- Deficit + cardio raises **systemic fatigue**. Use **shorter mesocycles** or **earlier deloads** when joints, mood, sleep, or performance crater for several sessions-not only when the spreadsheet says so.
- A deload can mean **fewer sets**, **lighter loads**, or **easier RPE targets** for a week; match the person.

---

## Integration with cardio and nutrition

- **Same-day cardio and weights:** spacing and which session comes first are covered in `cardio-exercise.md`-read that when programming the week.
- **Protein and calories** anchor retention-see `diet.md`.

## Summary

| Lever              | On a fat-loss program, bias toward…                                      |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Lifting goal       | Muscle retention, quality reps, sustainable frequency                    |
| Volume             | Enough to signal muscles; avoid piling past what you can recover from    |
| Compounds          | Protected; RPE-capped, not daily maxouts                                 |
| Progression        | Small steps; realistic expectations in a deficit                         |
| Cardio / diet      | Use `cardio-exercise.md` and `diet.md`; lifting does not replace them   |
| Exercise selection | `bodybuilding.md` + `list_exercises`                                   |
