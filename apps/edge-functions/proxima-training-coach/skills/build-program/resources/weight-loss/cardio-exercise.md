# Weight Loss: Cardio and Exercise

Principles below align with Greg Doucette’s *Circle Diet*, his cookbooks, and his general coaching philosophy on fat loss and training. They are **food for thought or places to start**, not dogmatic rules to follow to the letter.

---

## Cardio

**Do cardio** for fat loss: a typical cardio session burns more calories per unit time than a typical weight-training session for most people.

- **Minimum:** aim for at least **~150 minutes per week** of cardio.
- **Fat-loss focus:** you can work up to **~300 minutes per week** when losing fat is the primary goal.

Adjust modality and intensity to what you can recover from and stick to.

---

## Build muscle to support metabolism

**Resistance training matters** for long-term fat loss and body composition. Lean mass costs more energy to maintain than fat mass, so carrying more muscle generally supports higher energy expenditure at rest and in daily life. Do not drop lifting just to “do more cardio.”

---

## Same-day cardio and weights

When you do **both** cardio and lifting on the same day:

- **Separate the sessions by at least ~8 hours** when possible.
- **Do whichever session matches your priority goal first** (e.g. if fat loss is the focus, that might mean cardio first; if strength or muscle is the focus, lifting first-match the plan to the person’s priority).
